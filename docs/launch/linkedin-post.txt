A useful AI assistant can quickly become something an entire team depends on. What should happen when that assistant becomes a critical workload?

In this article, I share why I created AI App Studio—an experimental, open-source framework for turning Custom GPT configurations, or a fresh set of instructions, into standalone AI applications with a familiar chat experience.

I explore the journey from end-user assistant to dedicated application, and the engineering needed for production: observability, continuous evaluations, logging, security, and support.
